Pizza Recipe

Home

This recipe was from a friend who loves to cook. It is a simple recipe that is easy to follow and makes a delicious pizza.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups warm water
  • 2 tsp honey
  • 2 tbsp yeast
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4-5 cups flour

Instructions

  1. Combine warm water and honey in a large bowl.
  2. Stir in yeast and let it sit for 5 minutes until foamy.
  3. Add salt and olive oil to the mixture.
  4. Gradually add flour, one cup at a time, until a soft dough forms.
  5. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  6. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let it rise in a warm place for 1-2 hours until doubled in size.
  7. Preheat oven to 475 degrees F (245 degrees C).
  8. Roll out the dough and place on a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  9. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  10. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  11. Add your favorite toppings (pepperoni, mushrooms, etc.).
  12. Bake in the preheated oven for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
  13. Remove from oven and let cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
